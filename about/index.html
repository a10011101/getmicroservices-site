About GetMicroservices

We are a done-for-you automation agency specialising in n8n workflows, AI agents, and business process automation for small businesses and agencies.

What We Do

GetMicroservices builds production-ready automations that eliminate manual work, connect your tools, and power AI-assisted workflows. We work primarily with small businesses, e-commerce operators, and digital agencies who want automation without hiring a full-time developer.

Our most common projects:

  • n8n workflow automation -- multi-step, API-connected, self-hosted
  • AI agents for customer support, lead qualification, and content operations
  • CRM and data integrations (HubSpot, Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets)
  • Telegram, Slack, and WhatsApp bots for teams and customers
  • Invoice and document processing pipelines
  • Lead generation and qualification automations

Why n8n

n8n is the backbone of almost everything we build. It is open-source, self-hostable, and far more capable than Zapier or Make for complex workflows. Self-hosting means no per-execution pricing, full data control, and the ability to run LLM-powered logic inside workflows.

We run n8n on client infrastructure (GCP, AWS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean) so you own your automations completely. No vendor lock-in.
