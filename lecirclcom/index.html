What We Offer

01

Private Shopping & Sourcing

A fully bespoke sourcing and shopping service covering fine jewellery, luxury watches, fashion, art and collectibles. Cinderelha accesses private sales, appointment-only maisons, pre-market pieces and rare secondary market finds that are not available through standard retail channels. Whether a specific reference, a bespoke commission or a curated selection for an occasion, each engagement is built entirely around the client.

02

Concierge & Reservations

Restaurant reservations at the world's most sought-after tables, private member club introductions, theatre and event bookings, gallery previews and exclusive access arrangements. Cinderelha handles the calls, the waitlists and the relationships so clients do not have to. Hotel suites, private villa rentals, ground transfers and private aviation arrangements are managed with the same level of care. Available across time zones, seven days a week.

03

Personal Styling & Wardrobe

Complete wardrobe direction for clients who want to look considered at every occasion without the time investment. Services include wardrobe editing and organisation, occasion dressing, capsule wardrobe building, personal shopping accompaniment and ongoing style management. Suitable for executives, public figures and internationally mobile individuals who require consistent, high-quality styling without overhead.
